Privacy

Roadster Loop is a browser game. It does not ask for your name, email or any account, and it does not send your gameplay to a server.

What is stored

Your best score is saved in your browser's local storage under the key roadsterLoopBest. It stays on your device. Clearing your site data removes it.

Third-party content

The game loads the three.js library from cdnjs (Cloudflare). Your browser contacts that host to download the file, which means it can see your IP address and standard request headers.

Advertising

This site is set up to use Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar identifiers to serve and measure ads, including personalised ads where the law and your choices allow it.
